Every journal entry balances: total debits equal total credits, so the books are never out of balance.
Payroll equals ledger, every month: what payroll says the company paid matches what was posted in the accounts.
Receivables equal open invoices: the money customers owe in the ledger matches the unpaid invoices.
Payroll employees exist: no salary is paid to someone who isn’t in the employees table.
Nobody is paid after leaving: no payroll row starts after an employee’s exit_date.
